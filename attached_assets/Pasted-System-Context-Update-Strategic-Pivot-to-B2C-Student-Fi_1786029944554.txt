System Context Update: Strategic Pivot to B2C (Student-First)
For this current task, we are executing a major strategic pivot for MedQrown MedEazy. Previously, the platform was being built as a B2B, administrator-focused tool (institutional exam management). We are now pivoting to a B2C, student-first, product-led growth model.

We want to attract medical students directly by offering a gamified, competitive clinical study hub. Institutions will be targeted later. The entire psychology of the landing page must change from "functional B2B administrative software" to a "high-energy, competitive clinical training ground."

5. The Core Features Grid
This replaces the old administrative features. Each gets a card with an icon and a punchy description.

AI Clinical Scenarios: Never just memorize a muscle again. Our AI automatically frames basic sciences within high-yield patient presentations to build your clinical instincts early.

Standoffs (Rapid Fire): Go head-to-head. Challenge a friend to a 1v1 or 2v2 clinical duel with a 60-second clock. Think of it like a rapid tactical chess match, but for clinical reasoning. The fastest accurate diagnosis takes the points.

Competitive Elo Ratings: Track your clinical reasoning over time. Watch your rating climb as you master complex mechanisms, conquer your cohort, and dominate the global leaderboard.

Peer-Hosted Lobbies: Create private exam rooms, set your own time limits, and test your knowledge against your specific study group.

The XP Grind: Win Standoffs and dominate lobbies to earn Clinical XP. Use your winnings to unlock advanced specialty cases, extra AI exam generations, and exclusive dashboard themes.

XP Store Access: Instantly purchase Clinical XP to unlock premium AI utility, specific clinical scenario packs, or exclusive visual customizations.
